The China subset supports the cross-pathogen and cross-regime validation in Section~\ref{sec:app-china}
(`code/china_covid_empirical.py`). Its 1,143 daily columns are aggregated to 157 weekly
province-weeks; the analysed window is 2020-01-25 to 2023-01-21, and rows enter when their own
week-to-week growth factor is at least 1.05 and $C_t \ge 5$. The series ends on 2023-03-09
because the JHU CSSE global feed stopped updating after China discontinued daily case reporting.

NHSN fields used:
- `Geographic aggregation` and `Week Ending Date`;
- `Total Influenza Admissions`, `Total COVID-19 Admissions` and `Total RSV Admissions`;
- the percentage of hospitals reporting each pathogen, which gives the reporting-completeness proxy p_hat.

Fifty-one jurisdictions (50 states and DC) are used; territories and national aggregates are dropped.

## Integrity checks performed

- **NHSN.**
  - Row count, column count and week range were read back after decompression; the values in the table above are those readings.
  - Primary analysis sample ($R_A$, 4 early-growth seasons, rule v2): N = 1,748 state-weeks across 51 jurisdictions with $C_t \ge 5$, yielding pooled affine intercept $a = 0.01495$ (0.0101–0.0206) and $b = 0.560$ (0.362–0.769). The $G$ renewal sample has N = 1,756 with $D_t \ge 5$.
  - Historical archival baseline: the initial 3-season calendar window verification yielded a = 0.099055 (against 0.099060 in preliminary unrevised runs, N = 1,267).
  - No early-window rows come from imputed zeros: 0 of 1,748 rows for $R_A$ and 0 of 1,756 rows for $G$.
- **Delphi.**
  - Every state request returned `result = 1` or reported no data.
  - Seven jurisdictions returned no rows. They are listed in the table above and are excluded from the laboratory proxy only.
  - Matched rows are restricted to the rule windows, giving N = 1,224 state-weeks in 44 states.
