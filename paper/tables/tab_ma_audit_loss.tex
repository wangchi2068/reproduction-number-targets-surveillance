200 synthetic panels per generator on the real 1748-row $R_A$ design; analyst scale $D$ = simulated $C_t$ and the filter $C_t \ge 5$ applied to each panel; $n$ retained and the $D$ quantiles are Monte Carlo means over panels. Count alignment: each run of consecutive weeks is level-anchored to the observed counts of that run, and a global scale per generator (0.797-0.844) sets the mean retained median $D$ to 45 (observed 45). The upper tail is not matched: the generator P90 of $D$ is 263--324 against the observed 389. Intervals are Monte Carlo 2.5--97.5\% ranges across panels; a / truth is the ratio of panel means with the range of panel ratios. Truth is the relative variance of the target $R_i$ on retained rows; for S6 it also includes the relative variance of the reporting-shock term. True-error intercept: affine fit of $((x-R_i)/\bar{x})^2$. Week-centred reduction = $1 - a_{\mathrm{week}}/a$. Observed a: primary bootstrap (2,000 draws). S6 (shock log-SD $0.122$): a / $v_R$ = 2.02 [1.71, 2.29]; true-error intercept / reporting-shock variance = 1.02 [0.83, 1.19]; lag-1 correlation of the shock term between consecutive rows $-0.50$ [$ -0.53$, $-0.46$].
