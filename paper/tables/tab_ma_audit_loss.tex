The calibration targets, which are not tests, are the median retained D (count scale 0.797-0.844, all generators) and, for the generators with heterogeneity (S1, S3, S4, S5, S6, S7a, S7b, S8), the Monte Carlo mean of a, which equals the observed a by construction. All other columns are tests. The upper tail of D is not matched (generator P90 263-324, observed 389). Post-stratifying retained rows to the observed deciles of D changes a / ($v_R$ + $v_H$) from 1.007 to 1.008 (S1), 1.115 to 1.079 (S4), 1.009 to 1.004 (S6) and 0.684 to 0.665 (S7a). $v_R$ is the relative variance of the target $R_i$ on retained rows, and $v_H$ the current-value error caused by reporting shocks (S6, S8 only). Ratios are ratios of panel means with the 2.5-97.5\% range of panel ratios; MC SE = SD/sqrt(panels). The true-error intercept is the affine fit of ((x - $R_i$)/$\bar x$)$^2$. Week-centred reduction = 1 - $a_{\mathrm{week}}$/a, not defined without transmission heterogeneity. The observed a uses the primary bootstrap (2,000 draws). Shock log-SD s = 0.122 (S6) and 0.133 (S8). Under S6 the true-error intercept / $v_H$ is 1.02 [0.83, 1.19]; lag-1 correlation of the shock term -0.50 [-0.53, -0.46]. The transmission relative SD implied by the observed a under each generator's a / $v_R$ ranges from 9\% to 19\%.
